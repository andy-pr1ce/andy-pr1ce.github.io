My method

The Price Leadership Cycle

A framework I developed to strengthen mission-driven institutions so they can serve more people, develop stronger leaders, and endure long beyond the tenure of any one leader.

01

Diagnose

Understand the true challenges by examining root causes: systems, incentives, culture, and leadership dynamics.

02

Clarify

Define the priorities that matter most, and make direction simple, visible, and widely understood.

03

Decide

Make disciplined decisions once sufficient information is available. Effective leadership requires timely decisions.

04

Align

Ensure boards, staff, volunteers, and stakeholders understand and support the direction.

05

Execute

Implement decisions with accountability, measurable outcomes, and disciplined follow-through.

06

Evaluate

Review results, learn from outcomes, and apply the lessons to the next diagnosis. Then the cycle repeats.

The Hive Leadership System

Strong institutions work like a healthy hive

The cycle is the engine. It keeps three structural layers healthy and aligned.

Mission

Direction and purpose

Guides strategy, priorities, and long-term impact. Every decision should serve it.

Systems

The honeycomb

Governance, financial models, decision processes, and operating discipline that create stability.

People

Leaders and teams

Staff, volunteers, and stakeholders who understand their roles and stay aligned around the mission.

Principles: Mission first · Clarity of strategy · Accountability · Stewardship · Integrity

The goal of leadership is not simply to manage an organization, but to leave it healthier, more capable, and more effective long after the current leader has moved on.
