My story

A learner and a builder

In 1982, a district executive walked into my elementary school classroom and invited the boys to join Cub Scouts. Had he not taken the time to visit that day, my life would almost certainly have followed a different path. I was the oldest of three boys in an enlisted Navy family, moving from state to state, and Scouting moved with us.

After a year in banking, I planned to "play Boy Scout" for a couple of years, earn an MBA, and get a "real" job. I quickly learned this is a real job, one that combines leadership, finance, operations, fundraising, marketing, and people development like few careers do. Like any enterprise, we put capital and labor to work. Our dividends are young people better prepared to lead, serve, and succeed.

Whether it's learning to fly fish, building a tube amplifier, or solving a hard organizational problem, I'm energized by making tomorrow better than today.
